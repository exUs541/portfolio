### **SAP Signavio Quick Switch** ⚡️

We've rolled out multiple updates, consolidating a host of improvements to make your Signavio experience smoother, faster, and more intuitive than ever before.

*   **🚀 Performance Unleashed:** We've implemented significant performance optimizations, including a debounced MutationObserver and enhanced loading feedback, ensuring a snappier and more responsive interaction.
*   **🗺️ Dynamic Minimap & Navigation:**
    *   **Draggable Minimap:** The minimap is now fully **draggable** and anchored to the bottom-right corner by default, giving you flexible control over your workspace.
    *   **Expand-All Activities:** A powerful new feature allows you to **expand all activities** with granular control over attribute visibility via a dedicated attribute picker, replacing the previous hover-hide mechanism.
    *   **Navigation Trail:** Enhanced navigation trail features help you maintain context and effortlessly traverse complex processes.
*   **⚙️ Streamlined User Interface & Settings:**
    *   **Intuitive Popup Settings:** Popup settings have been thoughtfully reorganized into **collapsible sections**, making them cleaner and easier to navigate. Process Steps settings are also simplified.
    *   **Quick Switch Toggle:** The 'quick switch' eye icon has been replaced with a more intuitive **toggle switch** for improved usability.
    *   **Bulk Attribute Management:** Introducing **bulk select/deselect** options for attributes, saving you valuable time when configuring visibility.
*   **🛡️ Enhanced Reliability & Stability:**
    *   Addressed and fixed issues with stale attribute lists, silent authentication timing failures, and improved the reliability of the "Expand all" feature across different user environments.
    *   Minor UI refinements and fixes for user/group counts and language strings ensure a polished experience.
