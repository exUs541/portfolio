### **SAP Signavio Enhancement Suite**

This week brings a significant leap forward for the **SAP Signavio Enhancement Suite**, with a focus on enterprise manageability, user control, and expanded accessibility. These updates ensure a more robust, flexible, and intuitive experience for everyone.

*   ✨ **Expanded Accessibility:** Now officially available on the **Microsoft Edge Add-ons Store**, broadening reach and simplifying IT deployments with an updated deployment guide.
*   🚀 **Refined User Experience:** Enhanced hover feedback on collapsible setting groups makes navigation within the extension's settings even more intuitive and user-friendly.
*   🔐 **Unwavering User/Admin Control:** We've reinforced complete user and administrator control over critical features like **Quick Switch New Tab** and **Workspace Stats** within the managed storage schema, ensuring full autonomy.
*   🏢 **Robust Enterprise Policy Support:** Introduced comprehensive **Enterprise Policy Support** for IT administrators, enabling streamlined enforcement and UI locking for consistent and secure enterprise deployments via `chrome.storage.managed` schema.
*   ⚙️ **Agile Development Precision:** Adopted a more granular 4-component micro-versioning system, underscoring our commitment to precise, continuous, and agile development.
