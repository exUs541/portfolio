### **SAP Signavio Enhancement Suite** 🛠️

For our power users leveraging the **SAP Signavio Enhancement Suite**, get ready for a significant leap in usability and control. We've rolled out multiple updates culminating in version **1.9.1.8**, packed with features designed to streamline your Signavio workflows:

*   **Enhanced Dictionary Navigation:** We've made **Dictionary top-level categories collapsible** (v1.9.1.5), and in the latest update, refined the controls with intuitive **expand/collapse all** options (v1.9.1.8). Navigate complex dictionaries with unprecedented ease! 📚
*   **Granular Control & Privacy:** Your settings, your rules! **Quick Switch New Tab** and **Workspace Stats** are now 100% user/admin controlled in our managed storage schema (v1.9.1.2), ensuring full autonomy over your data and workflows. 🔒
*   **Smarter User Interface:** Experience smoother interactions with **enhanced accordion summary hover feedback** for collapsible setting groups (v1.9.1.3). Small details, big impact on your daily UX! ✨
*   **Broader Accessibility & Deployment:** The **SAP Signavio Enhancement Suite** is now officially available on the **Microsoft Edge Add-ons Store** (v1.9.1.4, with v1.9.1.8 ensuring seamless publishing), alongside comprehensive **IT deployment guides** (v1.9.1.4) for streamlined enterprise rollout. 🌐
