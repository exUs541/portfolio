Browser extensions

My extensions (Search Filter & Site Blocker, TabMaster, WebNote, YT Filter, Sync Scroll Pro, Kleinanzeigen Plus and the SAP Signavio Enhancement Suite) do not collect, store or transmit your personal data, browsing history or search queries to me or to any server operated by me. They have no backend. Everything they do happens inside your browser.

What they store

  • On your device: settings, blocklists, rules, notes, drawings and highlights are kept in the extension's browser storage.
  • Chrome sync: some extensions keep settings in Chrome's sync storage. If you have turned on Chrome sync, Google copies that data between your devices through your Google Account. I have no access to it. You can turn this off in Chrome.
  • Exports: where an extension offers a download or export (for example notes, images or an Excel file), the file is created on your device and saved where you choose.

Google Fonts

The pop-up or settings pages of several extensions load the Outfit or Inter font from Google Fonts when you open them. TabMaster does the same when it shows its own dialog on a web page. Because of this, Google receives your IP address and browser details, like on any site that uses Google Fonts. No content from the pages you visit is included. See Google's privacy policy.

Android apps

BeFit account required

BeFit lets you do short exercise challenges, record them on video and have friends confirm them. That needs an account and servers, so it is the one product of mine that processes personal data. In short:

  • Data: e-mail address, display name and profile picture, exercise videos and photos, points and training history, group chats, optional weight, and a city name if you opt in to city rankings.
  • Location: your GPS position is read once, only when you ask for it, turned into a city name and then discarded. Only the city is stored.
  • Where: data is stored with Supabase in the EU (Paris, France). Videos and photos are deleted automatically after 90 days, chat attachments after 180 days.
  • Advertising: the Android app shows ads through Google AdMob, personalised only with your consent, which you can change at any time. BeFit Pro and the web version have no ads. No other trackers or analytics.
  • Your control: you can delete your account and download a copy of your data in the app.

The full BeFit policy, including legal bases, recipients and retention, is the one that applies to BeFit: befitnow.app/privacy.

Focus Town

Focus Town is a single-player focus timer. It works fully offline: it does not request internet access or any other sensitive permission, has no account, no ads and no analytics. Coins, shop items, statistics and streaks are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone and are removed when you uninstall the app.

VR apps for Meta Quest

My VR apps run entirely on the headset. In both apps:

  • No internet access. The apps do not request the internet permission and make no network connections.
  • No accounts, ads, analytics or purchases, and no social features or chat.
  • No microphone, camera images, location, contacts or storage access. The apps do not use your name, profile or friends list.
  • Local data only. What the apps save stays in their private storage on the headset and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Nestwise Meta Quest 3 / 3S / Pro

Nestwise is a room planner in mixed reality: you place virtual furniture in your real room. This section applies to the native app, version 1.0.0 and later.

  • Saved on the headset Your designs (furniture, walls, sizes, colours), up to three saved drafts per room and an automatic last-session save, your settings, prices you edited, and a description of the walls, windows and doors of rooms you imported, so a design can be placed back in the right spot.
  • Room scan When you import your room, or open the app in a room with a saved design, Nestwise asks the Quest system for the room model (walls, windows, doors, floor, ceiling) created with the headset's Room Setup. The system asks for your permission first. The app uses this data only on the headset and never transmits it.
  • Passthrough The real-world view is shown by the system. The app cannot access the camera images.
  • Permissions Spatial data (room model), the spatial-anchor interface that goes with it, and the standard OpenXR permissions. Nothing else.
  • Delete Delete a design in the app (Settings), or uninstall the app, to remove everything.

Perspectives Meta Quest 3 / 3S

Perspectives is an empathy app that simulates, in a simplified way, how people can perceive the world: mental health conditions, sensory conditions, drug effects and a cartoon world.

  • Saved on the headset Only your settings: effect strength, session length, switches for motion effects, flashing effects, captions, smooth turning, small tasks and the screen filter, and whether you have read the safety notice.
  • Hand tracking If you play with your hands, the headset reports the position of your hands and fingers so you can point, pinch and grab. The app uses this live, only on the headset, and does not store or transmit it. Controller input is handled the same way.
  • Health information The app does not ask for, record or report any health information. Which experiences you choose is not saved. Voices, sounds and music are generated on the headset; nothing is recorded.
  • Not medical Perspectives is not a medical device and cannot diagnose or treat anything. If something in it affects you, use the pause button at any time and talk to a professional if you need support.
  • Age The app is not meant for children.

This website

This site (exus541.github.io/portfolio) is a static site on GitHub Pages. It has no user accounts, no analytics and no advertising cookies.

  • Hosting: GitHub, Inc. (USA) hosts the site and, like any web server, processes your IP address and request details in server logs. See GitHub's privacy statement. I do not see these logs.
  • Fonts and icons: the pages load fonts from Google Fonts and icons from the unpkg CDN. Your browser therefore sends your IP address to these providers when a page loads.
  • Badges: some pages show status badges loaded from img.shields.io.
  • Feedback forms: if you send an idea or bug report on the feedback page, what you type (name, optional e-mail, message and technical details) is sent through Formspree (Formspree, Inc., USA) to me. I use it only to answer you and to improve my tools, and I keep it only as long as that takes. Please do not enter sensitive information. Some pages also link to forms on Google Forms.
  • Roadmap votes: your up/down votes on the roadmap page are stored only in your browser (local storage) and are not sent to me.
  • External links: links to the Chrome Web Store, GitHub, YouTube, LinkedIn and Patreon lead to services with their own privacy policies. They only receive data once you open them.
