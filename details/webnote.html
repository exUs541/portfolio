WebNote

Annotate, highlight, and capture your research directly on the web.

Overview

WebNote turns your browser into a powerful research tool. Annotate websites with sticky notes, highlight critical information, and use freehand drawing tools to mark up pages. All your annotations are saved locally, so they are ready whenever you return to the site.

Key Features

  • Sticky Notes: Add floating notes to any part of a webpage.
  • Text Highlighting: Mark important sentences with various colors.
  • Drawing Tools: Draw circles, arrows, or sketches directly on the site.
  • Local Storage: Everything is saved in your browser, ensuring privacy and speed.
  • Research Dashboard: View and manage all your notes in one centralized place.

Perfect for Research

Whether you're a student, developer, or researcher, WebNote helps you keep track of your findings without leaving the browser.
