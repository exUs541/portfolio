Kleinanzeigen Plus

Erweitert Kleinanzeigen um Ad-Filtering, Image-Previews, Vorlagen, Preissuche uvm. The essential power-user companion for Germany's largest classifieds platform.

Overview

Kleinanzeigen Plus supercharges your experience on Kleinanzeigen.de — Germany's most popular classifieds platform. Filter out unwanted ads, instantly preview listing images, find deals by price range, and save time with message templates. Everything you need for a cleaner, faster browsing experience.

Key Features

  • Ad Filtering Block specific keywords, categories, or sellers to clean up your search results.
  • Image Previews See listing images without opening each listing individually.
  • Price Search Filter listings by exact price range for precise deal-hunting.
  • Message Templates Save and reuse message templates to contact sellers faster.
  • Enhanced UI Additional information and quality-of-life improvements on every listing page.

Privacy First

All settings and preferences are stored locally on your device. No data is collected, shared, or transmitted to any external server. The extension only activates on Kleinanzeigen.de pages.
