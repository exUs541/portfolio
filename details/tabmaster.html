TabMaster

Master your browser workspace with custom tab rules.

Overview

TabMaster is a lightweight extension that lets you customize your browser tabs. Rename them or change their icons using emojis or images based on simple URL rules. Perfect for distinguishing between multiple development environments or organizing research.

Key Features

  • Dynamic Renaming: Override tab titles automatically based on URL patterns.
  • Custom Icons: Use emojis or upload your own images as tab icons.
  • Flexible Rules: Match URLs using "Starts with", "Contains", or exact matches.
  • SPA Support: Works perfectly on modern web apps (React, Vue, etc.) without reloads.
  • Rule Dashboard: Easy-to-use interface to manage all your customizations.

Workflow Integration

Easily add rules for the current page directly from the extension popup. It's designed to be fast, intuitive, and out of your way.
