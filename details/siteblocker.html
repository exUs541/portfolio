Clean up your search results and stay focused on what matters.

Overview

Search Optimizer (SiteBlocker) is a powerful browser extension designed to declutter your Google Search experience. It gives you full control over what you see, allowing you to block unwanted domains, hide distracting modules, and use advanced search operators with ease.

Key Features

  • Domain Blocker Block any domain directly from search results with one click.
  • Ad-Free Experience Automatically hides sponsored posts and annoying ads.
  • Module Control Hide "People also ask", "Videos", and "Images" blocks to clean up the UI.
  • Infinite Scroll Seamlessly load the next page of results as you scroll.
  • Advanced Operators Quick access to filetype, site, and date filters.

Privacy First

Search Optimizer respects your privacy. All your settings and blocklists are stored locally on your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data or search history.
