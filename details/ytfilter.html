YT Filter

Take back control of your YouTube feed and focus on what matters.

Overview

YT Filter is designed for power users who want a cleaner, more personalized YouTube experience. Easily hide channels that don't interest you and filter out distracting content to create a focused viewing environment.

Key Features

  • Channel Blocker Permanently hide channels from your recommendations and search.
  • Content Filtering Filter videos based on keywords or categories.
  • Clean Interface Remove clutter from the YouTube homepage and sidebar.
  • Sync Settings Keep your filters consistent across all your devices.
  • Lightweight Built to run fast without slowing down your browser.

Focused Learning

Turn YouTube into a distraction-free learning platform by filtering out entertainment and focusing on educational content.
